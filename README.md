**🏰 Medieval Fantasy Kingdom — Personal Game Project**

*Inspired by the good old days of playing Social Empires on Facebook.*

I'm building my own medieval fantasy kingdom strategy game, combining kingdom building, army management, and monster fusion mechanics.

The goal is to create a fantasy world where players build their kingdoms, command armies of soldiers and monsters, and discover new creatures through a unique fusion system.

### 🔮 Meet the Soul Mixer

**Soul Mixer** is the core mechanic of the game.

Combine two monsters to create a different monster, opening up possibilities for discovering new creatures and building unique armies.

🧬 **The idea behind it**
- 🐲 Fuse two monsters to discover another creature
- ✨ Explore different monster combinations
- 🛡️ Build an army with diverse monster abilities
- ⚔️ Use your creatures alongside soldiers to defend your kingdom and attack enemies

### ⚔️ The Vision

- 🏰 Build and expand your own medieval fantasy kingdom
- 🪵 Gather resources and develop your buildings
- 🛡️ Recruit soldiers and raise powerful monsters
- 🔮 Discover new monsters through the Soul Mixer
- ⚔️ Combine your forces to defend and conquer
